Rejected: K=50 (lighter), shrinking only <100 PA (discontinuity).

- **2026-09-30 — Hitter tiers = two tiers, above / below league median adj-xwOBA (PA-weighted, per season: .318 in 2025, .315 in 2026).** Only split where every group has ≥26 BF (100+ pitches) per tier (fresh starter 101/26, relief 31/30); cutpoint is league-defined so it doesn't move with Sasaki's sample. - **2026-09-30 — Fresh-starter group is defined at the PA level: every PA that begins within a start's first 15 pitches, played out in full (127 BF, ~503 pitches).** The chain ends in a walk (link d), which only exists at PA end; truncating at pitch 15 would cut PAs off before their outcome. Rejected: pitch-level for pitch metrics + PA-level for walks (group membership would differ by metric).
- **2026-09-30 — Link (a) metric: Putaway% (K ÷ two-strike pitches) primary; two-strike whiff% (whiffs ÷ two-strike swings) secondary.** Putaway% is Savant-standard, uses the largest unit (relief: 64 two-strike pitches), and counts called and swinging K. Whiff% isolates stuff: similar whiff% + lower putaway% → approach/command failure; both lower → stuff failure (against H1). Rejected: either alone, PA-level non-K rate (smallest n, overlaps link d). Relief n is thin (~32 two-strike pitches per hitter tier).
- **2026-09-30 — Link (b) out-of-zone definition: Savant `zone` column, zones 11–14 = out of zone.** Standard behind published O-Swing%; accounts for batter zone height. Rejected: custom rulebook-zone calc from plate_x/plate_z (non-comparable).
- **2026-09-30 — Link (b) metric: expansion gap = out-of-zone% in 0-2/1-2/2-2 minus out-of-zone% in <2-strike counts, within each group; 3-2 excluded.** Measures expansion relative to his own baseline, so general wildness doesn't masquerade as expanding. 3-2 excluded because pitchers return to the zone there; it's handled in link (d). Cost: relief 2K side = 52 pitches (~26 per hitter tier). Rejected: raw 2K out-of-zone% (with or without 3-2), gap including 3-2.
- **2026-09-30 — Link (c) split into two metrics, both within hitter tier, 0-2/1-2/2-2 only (3-2 excluded, handled in d): (c-i) waste share = waste-band ÷ all two-strike out-of-zone pitches [command: are misses non-competitive?]; (c-ii) chase rate on chaseable pitches = swings ÷ shadow+chase-band pitches [stuff/deception: do competitive pitches get swings?].** Separates the command vs. stuff explanations for "hitters don't bite." Bands = Savant attack zones (% of center-to-edge distance: heart <67, shadow 67–133, chase 133–200, waste >200), computed from plate_x/plate_z and sz_top/sz_bot (close approximation, not Savant's published field). Cost: relief c-ii ≈ 25 chaseable pitches (~12 per tier); report with interval. Rejected: three bands (4–8 pitch cells), unbanded chase rate (can't separate command/stuff), pooling tiers for (c).
- **2026-09-30 — Link (d) metric: two-strike walk rate = walks ÷ PAs reaching two strikes (primary), reported alongside overall walk rate and the share of walks that pass through two strikes.** Primary matches the chain exactly and uses every PA that entered it (relief 35 PAs). The companion numbers show how much of the walk problem the chain can explain at all (3-0/3-1 walks never reach two strikes). Rejected: 3-2 walk rate (relief n=10), overall BB% as primary (not the chain).
- **2026-09-30 — Walks = unintentional BB only.** IBB is a managerial decision, not a command failure; HBP counts as a PA but not a walk (not the "hitter takes out-of-zone pitches" chain).
- **2026-09-30 — Link (e) outcome = official runs charged (R) per start, from game logs.** Includes runners he left on who scored after he exited (often walked runners), which Statcast's on-mound run count misses. Rejected: ER (errors erase preceding walks' cost), Statcast on-mound runs (undercounts).
- **2026-09-30 — Good start = 0–2 R, bad start = 3+ R.** Keeps the "3+ runs" line, avoids the quality-start overlap (QS allows 3 ER), and splits roughly evenly. Rejected: continuous runs, QS vs. not (mixes in length). Only 1 scoreless start, so "scoreless" can't be its own category.
- **2026-09-30 — Link (e) explanatory metrics per start: walk rate (unintentional BB ÷ BF) and xwOBA on contact.** Rates, not counts, because bad starts are shorter. xwOBA on contact isolates contact quality from fielding/luck. Rejected: raw BB count (length-biased), hard-hit% (ignores launch angle).
- **2026-09-30 — Exclude spring training (game_type S) from all analysis.** (User decision.) It's a warm-up for pitchers and batters alike: inconsistent competition and effort. Sasaki had no spring relief outings, so it wouldn't help the thin ceiling sample anyway.

### Below: Claude's recommendations, applied by default per user instruction (2026-09-30). User reviews and may override any of them.

- **#4 Filters.**
  - Sasaki: all 2025–26 regular-season + postseason games. Starts = appearances beginning in the 1st inning; everything else = relief (ceiling). No special IL handling beyond that grouping.
  - 2026 postseason relief is **held out** of the ceiling and reserved for grading the projection (clean out-of-sample test).
  - Yamamoto benchmark: his 2024–26 regular-season + postseason **starts** only, split into fresh-starter / rest-of-starts with the same rules. His few relief outings are dropped (too few to form a group).
  - Excluded rows: intentional-walk PAs (entirely), pitchouts, pitches missing location. No pitch-type grouping needed: no H1 metric is split by pitch type.
  - No minimum-sample exclusions; every estimate is reported with its n, and cells under 30 are flagged "low n".
- **#3 add-on — swing/whiff definitions.** Whiff = swinging_strike, swinging_strike_blocked, missed_bunt. Swing = whiffs + foul, foul_tip, foul_bunt, bunt_foul_tip, hit_into_play. Foul tips count as contact. "Chaseable" = out of zone (zone 11–14) AND in the shadow or chase band.
- **#5 Method.**
  - Each metric is computed within each hitter tier, then combined into a **tier-standardized** value (a simple 50/50 average of the two tiers, which matches the league mix because the tiers are split at the median).
  - Uncertainty: **cluster bootstrap by game** (10,000 reps). Games are resampled, not pitches, because command varies by day and pitches within a game aren't independent. Relief games and start games are resampled separately; fresh and rest-of-starts share the same resampled start games, so their difference CI is valid.
  - Contrasts reported: all starts vs. relief (primary), relief→fresh starter (role/effort), fresh→rest of starts (fatigue). Yamamoto groups are shown as the benchmark.
  - Link (e), per start: bad-minus-good difference in mean BB% and in mean xwOBA-on-contact, each with a bootstrap CI over starts, plus a standardized difference (diff ÷ pooled SD) so the two can be compared on one scale. Secondary: logistic regression of bad start on standardized BB% + xwOBAcon together (31 starts, so treated as a rough check).
  - No claims rest on p-values.
- **#6 Pre-registered thresholds (starts vs. relief, tier-standardized).** Verdict per link: **Supported** = 95% CI of difference excludes 0 in the predicted direction AND point estimate ≥ the meaningful difference; **Contradicted** = CI excludes 0 in the opposite direction; otherwise **Inconclusive**.
  - (a) putaway% lower in starts by ≥5 pts. 2K whiff% (stuff check): "stuff not shown to decline" if the difference is < 5 pts; "stuff declined" if starts are lower by ≥5 with CI excluding 0.
  - (b) expansion gap > 0 in starts (he does expand); descriptive, no between-group direction predicted.
  - (c-i) waste share higher in starts by ≥10 pts. (c-ii) chase rate: if lower in starts by ≥5 pts with CI excluding 0, that points to stuff/deception (H4), not command.
  - (d) two-strike walk rate higher in starts by ≥4 pts.
  - (e) bad-minus-good BB% ≥ 3 pts with CI excluding 0, AND BB%'s standardized difference exceeds xwOBAcon's.
- **#7 Visualization.** (1) Headline "chain ladder": one panel per link, dot + 95% CI for Sasaki relief / fresh starter / rest of starts, with Yamamoto fresh/rest in gray. (2) Link (e) scatter: each start's BB% vs. xwOBAcon, colored good/bad. (3) Supporting: locations of two-strike out-of-zone pitches with attack-band outlines, relief vs. starts.
- **#8 Falsification.** H1's chain fails if: (e) contact quality separates bad starts more than walks do, or the BB% gap isn't meaningful; OR (a) 2K whiff% is meaningfully lower in starts (stuff, not command); OR (c) waste share is similar but chase rate is lower in starts (hitters reading good pitches → H4). If links hold only in rest-of-starts and not in fresh-starter, that points to fatigue (H5) rather than role.
